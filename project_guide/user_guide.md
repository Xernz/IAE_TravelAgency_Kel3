# User Guide: Travel Agency System

Welcome to the Travel Agency System! This guide will help you understand and use all major features as an end user.

## 1. Registration & Login
- Navigate to the registration page and fill in your email, password, name, and phone number.
- After registering, log in with your credentials.

## 2. Searching for Travel Options
- Use the dashboard to search for:
  - **Flights:** Enter origin, destination, and date.
  - **Hotels:** Enter location, check-in, and check-out dates.
  - **Trains:** Enter origin, destination, and date.
  - **Local Travel:** Enter location and date for rides or tours.
- Search results will display available options with details.

## 3. Making Bookings
- Select your desired option from the search results.
- Fill in passenger/guest details as required.
- Confirm your booking; a summary will be shown.

## 4. Payments
- For unpaid bookings, click the "Pay Now" button in the "My Bookings" page.
- Select your payment method and complete the transaction.
- Payment status will update in real time.

## 5. Managing Bookings
- Go to **My Bookings** from the navigation bar.
- View all your bookings with status, details, and payment information.
- Modify or cancel bookings (where allowed) using the provided buttons.
- Download or print booking confirmations if needed.

## 6. Managing Your Profile
- Access your profile from the navigation bar.
- Update your name, phone number, or email.
- Changes are saved instantly with feedback.

## 7. Navigating the UI
- Use the navigation bar to access:
  - Home/Dashboard
  - My Bookings
  - Profile
  - Logout
- The UI is responsive and works on both desktop and mobile devices.
- Error and success messages are shown clearly for all actions.

## 8. Support & Troubleshooting
- If you encounter issues, check for error messages or try refreshing the page.
- For persistent problems, contact support with your booking ID or error details.

---

Enjoy seamless travel planning and booking with the Travel Agency System!
